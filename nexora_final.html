04 / How we work

Small team. Big attention.

Nexora started with a simple idea: businesses shouldn't need a massive agency budget to have a digital presence they're genuinely proud of.

We work closely with founders, local businesses, restaurants, creators and growing companies. You talk to the people doing the work — not three layers of people before your idea reaches the designer.

And if you don't need something, we'll say so. We'd rather solve the right problem than sell you another one.
